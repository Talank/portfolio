However large the problems in your life get, they are usually one of two kinds. Either they can be broken into smaller pieces, or there is a repeating shape hidden somewhere inside them. These twenty islands teach you to see those same two things.

Listen to one word now, because it comes back many times later. The word is [..]input size[..], and it means how big the work is. Cooking rice for one person and cooking rice for a hundred people is the same work, but the size is different.

The word interview makes many people tense. But the truth is that the questions asked in an interview are these same twenty shapes wearing different clothes.
