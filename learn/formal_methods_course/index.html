Formal Methods & Solvers

Interview prep for working with SMT solvers and theorem provers. It starts with SAT (DPLL, CDCL) and moves to SMT theories and Z3 in Python: bit-vectors, arrays, uninterpreted functions, quantifiers and optimisation. Then it covers symbolic execution, Hoare logic and verification conditions, bounded model checking, Dafny, and Lean 4 from propositions-as-types through tactics to proofs about real programs. It closes with how solvers are used in industry and research, including LLMs paired with formal methods.

Every lesson has the real mechanics, a One Piece story that acts the idea out (plus a sitcom scene), an interactive walkthrough, a code lab, a quiz, and the interview questions with strong answers. Flip Essentials → Full Depth in the header for the deeper material. There is also a 🌙 Nepali bedtime voyage that tells the whole syllabus as one night at sea.

How to use this

  1. Z3 first, Lean last. Z3 makes you comfortable stating properties as constraints, and Lean then asks you to prove them by hand. Parts 0–1 are the most commonly interviewed.
  2. In each lesson, read the concept and click through the interactive walkthrough. Do the Code Lab in the browser, then run it for real. Answer the interview questions out loud before you open them.
  3. Setup: pip install z3-solver, plus Lean 4 via elan (or the Lean web editor) and optionally the Dafny VS Code extension.
  4. Keep the Cheat Sheet open in a second tab, and finish with the timed Interview Drill.
  5. The night before: put on the 🌙 bedtime voyage.
