What this is

The whole course, thirty-nine lessons, standing about in a valley. Every keeper explains their idea out loud before asking you anything, and the mathematics region hands you the mathematics as a toy rather than a question — turn an arrow until the cosine reaches one, feel a slope in the fog, watch a rare disease drown in false alarms.

Nothing here is timed and nothing can be failed. A wrong answer is answered with an explanation and the question comes back later. Collect a pearl for each lesson you finish; the Groke opens her circle at twenty of them, and asks about anything at all.

Your progress here is kept separately from the dashboard — answering questions in a game is not the same as having studied the lesson, and this page will not tick anything off on your behalf.
